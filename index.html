FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about working with us? Find detailed answers below.

How does NorthPeak ensure campaign ROI?

We base all campaigns on quantitative client pipeline growth. During onboarding, we integrate end-to-end analytics tools to track organic and paid search channels down to actual purchases, form submissions, and contracts, optimizing campaigns weekly based on actual numbers.

What tools and frameworks do you use for Web Development?

We specialize in highly optimized vanilla systems, React, Vite, and Next.js for web projects, choosing headless setups (such as headless Shopify or Sanity CMS) to guarantee sub-second page loads, SEO optimization, and responsive design systems.

How long does a standard onboarding phase take?

Onboarding usually spans 1 to 2 weeks. During this window, we conduct technical audits, perform deep keyword research, construct custom visual profiles, hold briefing sessions, and gain analytics permissions before kickstarting any ad spends or coding assets.

Do you offer custom one-time project contracts?

Yes. While most clients scale with us via monthly growth retainers, we build one-time projects such as complete visual redesigns, software products design-to-launch campaigns, and custom SEO configurations with flat pricing models.

How do we receive updates and performance metrics?

You will receive bi-weekly progress summaries in plain English, a unified live metrics dashboard tracking lead counts, search visibility, and ROI, and a monthly brief alignment call with your dedicated Account Director.

Is there a minimum contract length for retainers?

No. We believe in earning our place month over month. Our monthly retainer services are based on a 30-day cancellation notice format, so you are never locked into restrictive multi-year contracts.
